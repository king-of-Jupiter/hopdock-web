## Prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original Halloween illustration for the right half of Hopdock SSH desktop-app website hero. Create a premium art-directed underground 2016 internet mixtape / haunted old PC game cover illustration, with tactile photocopied distressed cut-paper edges, halftone grain, rough black ink outlines and primitive low-poly late-1990s survival-game visual language. Main subject: a crooked chunky beige CRT computer on a battered desk in a tiny moss-covered graveyard, the monitor glows muted acid moss green with just a simple abstract terminal cursor (no readable code). A small bony skeleton wearing a dark hoodie sits casually at the computer, one hand on keyboard, a little black cat near the desk, an irregular pale moon, leafless branches and crooked gravestones behind. Composition is a single cohesive square cutout collage, striking silhouette, off-center perspective and playful eerie humor. Palette tightly restricted to charcoal black, warm ivory and desaturated moss green with tiny rusty burnt-orange details. Off-white paper flecks and green weeds. Atmospheric, raw and nostalgic, a handmade underground concert-poster collage, visually rich but readable when displayed 450px wide. Isolated overall silhouette on genuinely transparent background, no solid rectangle backdrop, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no UI mockup. Avoid polished corporate vector illustration, cute sheet ghosts, smooth gradients, glossy 3D, purple, pumpkins as main subject. This is decorative original artwork, not a replacement for any app screenshot.
